01 / OUR APPROACH

A picture is a memory.
A film is a memory brought to life.

The Vow Film Co. is a luxury wedding film company based in New England, founded by Darcy Lally. We bring a filmmaking background to wedding films, approaching each celebration with intention and care.

With professional cinema equipment and a considered approach to cinematography, we create visually striking films crafted to stand the test of time.

Our mission is not simply to help you remember how your wedding looked, but to revisit how it felt to be there. My grandfather used to say, “A picture is a memory,” and we like to think a film is a memory brought to life.

03 / WHY THE VOW

Thoughtful from start to finish.

Filmmaking

A background in narrative filmmaking shapes how we approach every wedding; from the way a story unfolds to the details which give it meaning. Rather than simply documenting the day's events, we bring an understanding of cinematic storytelling to the way each film is captured, structured, and edited.

Craft

Professional cinema cameras, carefully selected lenses, and intentional lighting give us greater control over the look and feel of each film. From the first frame to the final color grade and sound mix, every detail is considered to create a polished, cinematic result.

A Woman's Perspective

As a woman filmmaker, I bring a personal perspective to the way I approach each wedding and the people at its center. I value building trust with my couples, creating space for genuine moments to unfold, and capturing the individual relationships and details which make each celebration personal.
